Our foundation

Built on clarity,
driven by impact.

Founded in Seoul in 2009, Meridian began as a small team of economists and engineers who believed that the best decisions are made when complexity is stripped away—not hidden behind it.

Over fifteen years, we have grown into a multi-disciplinary practice spanning strategy, operations, and technology advisory. Our work spans the financial sector, manufacturing, and public institutions across Asia-Pacific and Europe.

We don't believe in frameworks for their own sake. We believe in asking better questions and staying with our clients long enough to see the answers matter.

Clarity over convenience
We surface the real problem before prescribing solutions. Rigorous diagnosis is non-negotiable.
Long-term partnership
Engagements are the beginning of a relationship, not a transaction. We measure success in years, not deliverables.
Informed independence
Our advice is not shaped by implementation revenue or affiliate interests. We say what we believe.
